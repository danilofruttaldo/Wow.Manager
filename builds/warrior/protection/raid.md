## Notes

- Mountain Thane Raid is a balanced offensive/defensive build; flex points include Bloodsurge, Enduring Alacrity, Thunderlord for defensive tuning.
- Colossus Raid offers the same flex points and an offensive variant for damage-focused encounters.
- Apex: Phalanx (offensive frontal cone on Thunder Clap and Shield Slam).
- Shield Block uptime: Heavy Repercussions at low haste, Into the Fray as haste scales.
