## Stat priority

Intellect > standard caster secondaries (Haste/Mastery/Crit/Vers). Tune secondaries to the active hero tree — Eclipse and Apex talents drive the priority across all variants.

## Notes

- KotG variant optimized for shorter pulls where burst dominates; scales well in lower keys.
- Elune's Chosen preferred at high keys for sustained Starfire damage during long pulls; cannot enter Solar Eclipse due to Lunar Calling.
- Delves variant drops group utility for single-target burst against bosses/mini-bosses.
- Integrate Dream Burst and Force of Nature for priority add damage when running KotG.
