## Stat priority

Intellect > standard caster secondaries (Haste/Mastery/Crit/Vers). Eclipse mechanics and Apex talents keep the priority stable across variants.

## Notes

- KotG ST: burst-focused via Incarnation: Chosen of Elune, best on priority damage fights.
- Elune's Chosen ST: top-tier sustained caster DPS; more flexible on multi-target encounters; simulates within 1% of KotG ST.
- Cleave/AoE variant runs Dream Burst + Force of Nature; ideal for mixed ST/add phases.
- Convoke variant substitutes Incarnation for situational CD timing; lower overall throughput.
