## Stat priority

Agility > Crit / Haste (vary by build) > Mastery / Versatility (situational). Unseen Predator Apex amplifies finishers across all variants.

## Notes

- DotC converts Ferocious Bite into AoE via Primal Wrath + Rampant Ferocity; Wild Slashes amplifies Swipe.
- Hunger for Battle smooths Energy between pulls; Berserk + Convoke provide burst windows.
- Bear Form access plus Frenzied Regeneration gives DotC notably better survival than Wildstalker.
- Wildstalker M+ alternative sacrifices ST tools to enable AoE; takes Double-Clawed Rake for easier DoT spread.
- Delves variant adds extra survival and burst tuning for unpredictable encounters.
