## Stat priority

Agility > standard tank secondaries; weight Haste/Mastery/Vers to taste. Both hero trees value rage-dumping into Ironfur for layered defensives.

## Notes

- EC M+: Thrash spam drives heals/absorbs; Moonfire fills when Thrash is on CD (no Mangle/Raze).
- DotC M+: burst AoE through massive Raze absorbs; preferred at lower keys before EC outscales.
- Dump all rage into Ironfur for defensive layers; rotation between ST and AoE stays fundamentally similar.
- Delves variant favors off-healing talents to support undergeared groups; Prowl skips trash to reach objectives.
