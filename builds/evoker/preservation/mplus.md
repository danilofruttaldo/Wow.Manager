## Notes

- Chronowarden emphasizes Tip the Scales cooldown management and Temporal Anomaly shield generation.
- Maximize Verdant Embrace casts to stack Haste via Reverberation and Primacy effects.
- Merithra's Blessing ensures Reversion bounces to multiple targets — keep it applied frequently.
- Flameshaper provides extra Dream Breath charges for reliable burst healing procs.
- Flameshaper feels smoother on farm content but is overall weaker than Chronowarden for progression.
